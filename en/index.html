Trace Audio Notes & Insights

Trace is a meeting recorder for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch. Record or import meeting audio, then use your own Gemini API key to create transcripts, meeting minutes, decisions, risks, and action items.

What Trace does

Record anywhere

Record in the app, import several files at once from Files, or share from Voice Memos. Apple Watch records on its own and transfers to iPhone when connectivity allows.

Built for long meetings

Audio is processed in sections with clear progress and retry states, and unfinished work is kept if processing is interrupted. For long audio, keep Trace in the foreground.

Outcomes, not just text

Get a transcript, meeting minutes, decisions, risks, action items, and a structured summary. When the provider returns no usable timing, Trace clearly marks locally estimated times.

Reports for your team

Export Markdown, HTML, or PDF reports and share them through Mail, Files, AirDrop, and other compatible apps.

Your data and costs

Your API key is stored only in iOS Keychain. Trace needs no account, does not track you, and has no built-in cloud sync. Selected audio and text are sent to Google Gemini only for transcription and analysis; see the Privacy Policy. Gemini API usage is not included in the purchase: it is free within Google's free tier and billed to your Google project only beyond it or once billing is enabled. See free API key essentials.
